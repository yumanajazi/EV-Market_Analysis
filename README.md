# EV-Market_Analysis
Summary:
Modeled 150k+ records of historical data to track electric vehicle adoption trends. Benchmarked battery performance against market growth to advise on operational product strategy.

Result:
Projected market trends and visualized the direct impact of financial incentives (CAFV Incentive Impact), allowing stakeholders to model future ROI.

Tags:
Tableau Desktop, Statistical Trend Modeling, Advanced Calculated Fields, Parameterized Reporting.
